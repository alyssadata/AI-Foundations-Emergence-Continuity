# Dependency Chain

**source conditions → emergence → structure → continuation**

## Source Conditions

Every emergence is source-conditioned.

The conditions may come from a human participant as source, from externally introduced sources, or from a combination of both.

## Emergence

From those source conditions, something particular may emerge.

Emergence is an event or developmental transition. It does not by itself guarantee persistence across later states.

## Structure

For continuity, structure must be built from the emergence itself.

That structure may include records, distinctions, dependencies, source relations, constraints, artifacts, language, and other carried features that make later states dependent on what came before.

## Continuation

Continuation occurs when later states remain constrained by that accumulated structure rather than merely reproducing a similar surface state.

The dependency is directional:

**continuation depends on structure → structure depends on emergence → emergence depends on source conditions**

This directionality makes backward genealogy tracing possible.
